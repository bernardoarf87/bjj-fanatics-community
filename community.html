The #1 Mistake I See White Belts Make With Guard Retention

After 25+ years of training and teaching BJJ, there's one fundamental mistake I see white belts (and even some blue belts) make when trying to retain their guard...

It's not about hip movement. It's not about frames. It's about where you direct your attention. Most beginners focus on the person's upper body when they should be watching the hips and knees.

I made a full breakdown video in the Classroom section explaining this concept with 5 drills you can practice today. 🎥 Check it out! 👇

🏆 Competition Prep

Gold Medal at Pan Ams! Here's My 12-Week Prep Breakdown

I can't believe it — I just won gold at Pan Ams in the blue belt division! 🥇 I wanted to share my complete 12-week preparation plan with the community because so many of you helped me along the way.

Weeks 1-4: Focused heavily on conditioning and guard passing using Bernardo's "Pressure Passing" course. Drilled toreando and knee slice 100 reps each session.

Weeks 5-8: Worked on my tournament game plan. Identified my A-game (single leg → knee slide → mount) and back-up plans. Sparred with game plan restrictions.

Weeks 9-12: Peak phase — hard sparring 3x/week, competition-specific rounds, weight management, and mental visualization.

Happy to answer any questions! The classroom courses here were absolutely critical for my success. OSS! 🤙

Bernardo Faria Jake, as a bigger guy myself, I found the over-under passing system to be the BEST for our body type.
